# Fun seeds

Seeds whose plant is an upgrade to the Garden rather than a crop to sell. Each has its own doc; this is the list.

| Seed | What it does | Doc |
|---|---|---|
| Popcorn | builds the Garden's screen | [[popcorn]] |
| Moonflower | beams up and closes a dome over the Garden, the light show's stage | [[moonflower]] |
| Spotlight Tulip | a stage light that sweeps the Garden to the music | [[spotlight-tulip]] |

All three are in the stalls. Grown, each raises the profit of what its Plot grows
([[economy#Upgrade bonus|economy → Upgrade bonus]]); each one's bonus, price and rarity are in
[[crops#Balance|crops → Balance]].

The Moonflower and the Spotlight Tulip are the **ARS seeds**: with the Garden's own props they make the
[[light-show]], which dances to the screen's sound. Weed is a crop with a twist rather than an upgrade ([[weed]]).
