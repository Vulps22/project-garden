# Fractured Isles — Tutorial Voice Script

## 0. Beta Notice
Hey, just a quick note to say the following tutorial is based on the assumption that the game is in a perfectly working state. Some features are unfinished and may not match the tutorial yet, but everything should be close enough.

## 1. Welcome
Welcome to Grow A Garden! You've been selected to win a free plot in our prestigious 'Fractured Isles' Allotment! To get started, pick up your deed from your desired plot and carry it into your brand new Hut!

## 2. First Thatch
Excellent! You have been awarded 10 Thatch to get you started! Use your thatch to buy a carrot from one of the barrels next to the Hut and plant it in your plot.

## 3. First Harvest
Check it out! You just grew your first carrot! Drop it off at the Sell Point marked by a yellow canvas covering to get your reward.

## 4. Selling Basics / Practice Goal
Congratulations! You just earned 15 Thatch! Now you have the basics of selling, let's practice a bit. Work your way up to 100 Thatch by planting Carrots, Turnips, and Pumpkins and selling them. I'll check back in when you reach 100 to teach you the basics of exploration.

## 5. Exploration Intro
Wehey! You've earned your first 100 Thatch! Ready to kick it up a notch? If you look out onto the horizon you'll see loads of floating islands and POIs out there just waiting to be explored. There you'll find new seed types, special upgrades, and all kinds of interesting easter eggs. Ready to go exploring? Just jump in the air and stretch out your arms and the wind will do the rest!

## 6. Flying (Oops Moment)
Look at that! You're flying! Okay, time for the trial by fire! Probably should have told you how to fly before I told you to jump, haha!

## 7. Flight Controls
Okay, so flight is all in the wrists. Rotate your hands up or down to ascend and descend. Rotate one up and one down to yaw, and rotate both up as far as you can to hover in place.

## 8. Trough-Shot Technique
Good! You'll notice that when you're ascending you slow down, and when you descend you speed up. You can use that to fly further by doing a trough-shot. Rotate your hands down for a few seconds, then come back up sharp — careful not to come up too sharp or you'll hit the breaks and lose all that momentum. If you do it right, you'll come up further than you went down!

## 9. Flying Wrap-Up
Spectacular! Okay, that's the basics of flying! Head on out into the world and see what amazing seeds you can find! Just remember you have to carry them home by hand before you can plant them!

## 10. First Fall (World Floor)
Oh! Hello! I forgot to warn you! If you fall too far, you'll hit our safety net and get teleported right back to the garden without any of the seeds you found along the way! Oh well, head on back out there and try again! There's hundreds of BuyPoints out there to find, and they recycle every 5 minutes, so whatever you lost, you're almost guaranteed to find it again! Have fun!

## 11. Falling Off an Island
Hey, it looks like you bounced off an island and are falling to your death... next time, you might try grabbing the rock faces. Wink wink.

## 12. Upgrade Seed Found
You've found an Upgrade! Congratulations! These are VERY rare seeds that improve your plot in one way or another, such as adding more space or solid fences you won't fall through. Just grow them in the garden and DON'T harvest them. If you harvest an upgrade, you will lose the upgrade. They do sell for good money too, by the way!

## 13. Buffing Seed Found
Ohh, that looks like a buffing seed! These seeds give you access to special features that boost productivity and the value of your seeds. Many of them behave differently, so grow it, experiment, and see if you can work out how it works. Have fun!

## 14. Hut Intro / Deed Scroll
This is your Hut. It's unique to you and the only place where you can manage access to your plot and your game settings. You see that floating scroll by the door? Other players can give you one of these if they want to work your plot with you. If you agree, simply carry the scroll into the hut and they will be added to the plot's deed list.

## 15. Player Added to Deed List
You have just added a player to your Plot's Deed List. They can now plant AND harvest anything on the plot. Be careful, that means they can also harvest your upgrades and buffs too! If at any point you change your mind, simply grab their deed off the wall and walk it out of the hut to revoke their access.
