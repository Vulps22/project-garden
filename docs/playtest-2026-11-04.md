## Open: found, not fixed

7. **Bought two island seeds signed "Carrot"; they were corn and tomato.** The sign shows the seed the stall adopted
   (`BuyPoint.SetCurrentSeed` → `StallDisplay.Show`), and a stall finds its seed by id (`ShopKeeper.StockAt`). If the
   runner cannot yet find a seed from inside its own spawn, the stall adopts nothing and keeps the last seed's sign,
   and its socket never holds the new seed. 2026-11-05, seen again (a tomato under a "Turnip" sign on an island, while
   Tirak hosted from the Garden): most likely a **left-over seed**. The cycle's discard reaches only seeds the master's
   client can find, so an old seed stayed in the socket after its stall restocked; the socket holds only the new
   stock, so taking the old one sent no purchase. **Fix built** (compiles clean, untested): left-over seeds end
   themselves, and the table carries item ids. [[shop]]
8. **Could not plant those seeds; they vanished when let go, while the master saw them in the air.** This client
   logs nothing about purchases (only the master does), so the master's log was needed; Addan's is gone (2026-11-05),

   never applied the purchase, so the seed was still `InShop` here, `CanBePlanted` was false, and letting go
   started the stall socket's recall to an island 300 m away, which reads as vanishing. Ties in with 7: a stall
   whose table entry disagrees with the seed in its socket; 7's fix may cover it.
9. **Flying failed after buying;** it only started once falling, and was unreliable after. A held seed counted as

## Economy

**Status (2026-11-04):** prices, weights and the island skew applied (compiles clean, untested in-world); the
values are in [[crops#Balance|crops → Balance]]. Vulps kept the pumpkin pinned at a lower profit and raised the rest,
with "very rare" rarity; two hands stay an early-game limit. Sinks not chosen yet. The analysis that led there:

A player had thousands of Thatch and nothing to spend it on. Findings:

- **Labour limits income, not slots.** A Plot has 24 slots; a player carries two things at a time. What matters is
  profit per hand-load (seed in, each fruit out).
- **The pumpkin dominates:** 50 profit on one fruit, 25 per hand-load, against 2.5–8 for most crops; pinned at every
  Garden, so supply never runs out. Pumpkins at home: about 7,000–15,000 Thatch an hour. Exploring islands: about
  1,700–1,900.
- **Island rarity is flat.** Island stalls roll a multiplier of 1–100 (about 50 on average), and `w·m^(1−w)` brings
  every crop below 0.5 to a similar share there: weed is about 13% of island stalls, not 3%. The pumpkin is the
  commonest island find.
- **Nothing buyable loses money when grown out;** two tomatoes and two weed return 684 on 200 spent. The loss in
  play was probably selling only the first tomato harvest, smoking blooms, produce through the floor, or leaving
  before the weed ripened.

The agent's first proposal, superseded by what was applied (sell per fruit; grow times unchanged):

| Seed | Buy | Sell now → new | Profit per plant | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carrot | 10 | 15 → 16 | 6 | floor, pinned |
| Turnip | 18 | 25 → 27 | 9 | floor, pinned |
| Pumpkin | 60 | 110 → **70** | 10 | floor, pinned |
| Tomato | 20 | 6 → **11** (×12) | 112 | island common |
| Corn | 25 | 13 → **25** (×3) | 50 | uncommon |
| Eggplant | 45 | 14 → **25** (×9) | 180 | uncommon |
| Weed | 80 | 30 → **80** (×9) | 640 | rare windfall |

- Spawn weights: tomato 0.8, pumpkin 0.35, corn 0.3, eggplant 0.15, tulip 0.1, weed 0.06, moonflower 0.04.
- Optional: take carrot, turnip and pumpkin out of island rolls, or cap the multiplier near 20.
- Result: pumpkins about 1,400–3,000 an hour; exploring about 4,400–4,600.
